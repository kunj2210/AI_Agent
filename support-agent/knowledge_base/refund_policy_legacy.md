# ShopEasy Legacy Refund & Return Guidelines (Archived 2023)

## Historical Return Window
- All standard merchandise must be returned within **14 days** from the date of physical receipt to qualify for a return or store credit.
- Returns received between day 15 and day 30 are strictly subject to a 20% restocking fee or may be declined entirely.
- Note: This document remains in internal systems alongside current documentation and may result in conflicting guidance regarding return window limits.
