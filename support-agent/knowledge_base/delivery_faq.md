# ShopEasy Delivery FAQ

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes **5 business days** from the date of dispatch. Express
delivery is available at checkout and typically arrives within **2 business days**.
Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on a business day are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed after the cut-off or on weekends are dispatched the next business day.

## How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking link via email and SMS.
You can also track your order by logging into your ShopEasy account, navigating to
**My Orders**, and clicking **Track Shipment** next to the relevant order.

## What happens if I miss my delivery?

Our courier partner will attempt delivery up to **3 times**. After the first missed
attempt, a notification is sent to your registered email and phone number. If all
3 attempts fail, the package is held at the nearest courier facility for **5 days**
before being returned to ShopEasy. In that case, please contact us to arrange
re-delivery — a re-delivery fee of ₹50 may apply.

## Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

Address changes are allowed within **1 hour** of placing the order. After that window,
the order enters processing and the address cannot be modified. To request a change,
contact ShopEasy support immediately with your order number.

## Do you deliver to all pin codes?

ShopEasy delivers to over 27,000 pin codes across India. To check serviceability,
enter your pin code on the product page before adding to cart. Remote or
hilly areas may have longer delivery timelines.
