# ShopEasy Billing FAQ

## What payment methods does ShopEasy accept?

ShopEasy accepts the following payment methods:
- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex)
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
- Net banking (all major Indian banks)
- ShopEasy Wallet
- Buy Now Pay Later via ZestMoney and LazyPay
- Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders up to ₹10,000

## My payment was deducted but the order wasn't placed. What do I do?

This sometimes happens due to a network timeout during checkout. In most cases,
the amount is automatically refunded to your original payment method within
**5–7 business days**. If you do not receive the refund after 7 days, please contact
our support team with your transaction reference ID and we will investigate.

## How do I get an invoice for my order?

Invoices are generated automatically and sent to your registered email address
within 24 hours of order confirmation. You can also download invoices at any time
from **My Orders → View Invoice**. GST invoices are available for all orders; please
ensure your GSTIN is saved in your account profile before placing the order.

## Can I pay in installments (EMI)?

Yes. EMI is available on orders above ₹3,000 when paying with eligible credit cards.
Select **EMI** as your payment method at checkout and choose a tenure of 3, 6, or
12 months. Processing fees and interest rates vary by bank and are displayed before
you confirm.

## What do I do if I was charged twice?

Double charges are rare but can occur during payment gateway errors. Please share
your order number and bank statement with our support team. We will verify and
initiate a reversal within **3 business days**.
