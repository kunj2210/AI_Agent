# ShopEasy Account Access

## I can't log in to my account. What should I do?

First, confirm you are using the email address associated with your ShopEasy account.
Passwords are case-sensitive, so check that Caps Lock is off. If you still can't log in,
click **Forgot Password** on the login page to reset it. A reset link will be sent to
your registered email and is valid for **30 minutes**.

If you no longer have access to the registered email, contact our support team with
a government-issued photo ID to verify your identity. We do not reset accounts
without identity verification for security reasons.

## My account has been locked. Why?

Accounts are temporarily locked after **5 consecutive failed login attempts** as a
security measure. The lock lifts automatically after **15 minutes**. If you believe
someone is trying to access your account without your permission, please contact
support immediately to freeze the account and start a security review.

## How do I change my registered email or phone number?

Log in to your account and go to **Profile → Edit Details**. Changes to your registered
email or phone require OTP verification on both the old and new contact details.
For security reasons, you can only make one contact-detail change every **30 days**.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)

ShopEasy supports 2FA via OTP sent to your registered mobile number. You can enable
or disable 2FA from **Profile → Security Settings**. We strongly recommend keeping
2FA enabled, especially if you have saved payment methods on the account.

## Account deletion

To permanently delete your account, go to **Profile → Delete Account**. Note that
deletion is irreversible. Any pending orders, wallet balance, or loyalty points will
be forfeited. The process takes up to **7 business days** to complete.
