# ShopEasy Technical Troubleshooting

## The ShopEasy website or app is not loading

1. Check your internet connection and try refreshing the page (Ctrl + R / Cmd + R).
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies, then reload.
3. Try a different browser or device to rule out a local issue.
4. Disable any VPN or ad-blocker, as these can sometimes interfere with our checkout.
5. If the problem persists, visit our **Status Page** at status.shopeasy.in to check
   for any ongoing outages or maintenance windows.

## Checkout is stuck or the payment page won't load

- Ensure JavaScript and cookies are enabled in your browser.
- Avoid using the browser's back button during checkout — always use on-screen buttons.
- If a payment attempt hangs for more than 2 minutes, close the tab and re-open the
  cart. Do not click **Pay** again until you have confirmed the first charge did not go
  through (check your bank app or statement).

## Images or product details are not displaying correctly

This is often a caching issue. Hard-refresh the page with **Ctrl + Shift + R**
(Windows/Linux) or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac). If the problem continues, try
accessing ShopEasy in an incognito/private window.

## The ShopEasy mobile app is crashing

1. Force-close the app and reopen it.
2. Make sure you are on the latest app version (check the App Store or Play Store).
3. Restart your device.
4. If crashes continue, uninstall and reinstall the app. Your account data is stored
   in the cloud and will not be lost.

## I'm not receiving OTPs or emails from ShopEasy

Check your spam/junk folder first. If our emails are landing there, mark them as
**Not Spam** to whitelist ShopEasy. For OTPs, ensure your registered phone number
is correct in **Profile → Edit Details** and that you have network signal.
