# ShopEasy Refund Policy

## General Refund Window

At ShopEasy, customer satisfaction is our top priority. If you are not completely
happy with your purchase, you may request a refund within **30 days of delivery**.
To be eligible, items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied
by a valid proof of purchase (order number or email confirmation).

Once your return is received and inspected, we will send you an email notification.
Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days and credited to your
original payment method. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return
is due to a ShopEasy error.

## How to Initiate a Refund

1. Log in to your ShopEasy account and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order and click **Request Refund**.
3. Choose the item(s), provide a reason, and submit.
4. Our team will review and respond within 2 business days.

For orders above ₹5,000, a customer service agent will contact you to verify
the return before approval.

## Sale and Discounted Items

**Please note:** Items purchased during a sale or at a discounted price follow a
stricter return policy. Sale items may only be returned within **7 days of delivery**.
After 7 days, sale items are considered final sale and are not eligible for a refund
or exchange, regardless of condition.

## Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds under any circumstance:
- Perishable goods (food, flowers)
- Digital downloads and software licences
- Personal hygiene products once opened
