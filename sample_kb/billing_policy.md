# ShopEasy Billing & Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
- We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
- We do not accept wire transfers, money orders, or cryptocurrencies.

## Duplicate Charges & Payment Authorizations
- Temporary pending authorization holds may appear on your bank statement upon checkout. These holds typically release within 3 to 5 business days depending on your issuing financial institution.
- In the event of a verified duplicate charge, customers must provide the Transaction Reference ID or billing statement snippet. Once verified by billing agents, duplicate charges are credited back within 48 business hours.
