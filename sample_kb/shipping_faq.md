# ShopEasy Shipping & Delivery FAQ

## Delivery Timeframes
- Standard Shipping: 3 to 5 business days across continental regions.
- Express Shipping: 1 to 2 business days for orders placed before 2:00 PM local time.
- Processing times: Orders require 24 to 48 hours for warehouse fulfillment prior to shipment.

## Order Tracking
- Once your package leaves our fulfillment facility, a carrier tracking link and number are sent via email and SMS.
- You can also view real-time delivery status by navigating to My Account -> My Orders -> Track Shipment.
- If an order has not arrived within 5 business days of dispatch, please contact support with your Order ID so an investigation can be opened.
