# ShopEasy Return & Refund Policy

## Standard Return Window
- Customers may return unopened, unused products in their original packaging within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Return shipping is free for items that arrive defective, damaged, or incorrect.
- For voluntary returns (e.g. buyer's remorse), a flat return shipping fee of $5.99 is deducted from the refund amount.

## Processing Timelines
- Once received at our warehouse, returns are inspected within 2 to 3 business days.
- Refunds typically appear on credit or debit card statements within 5 to 7 business days after approval.

## Exceptions & Non-Returnable Items
- Perishable goods, personalized/custom items, and clearance/final sale products are non-refundable.
- Orders exceeding $500 require human specialist approval and manual review before any return or refund can be processed.
