# ShopEasy Account Access & Security Policy

## Password Resets
- To reset your password, visit the login page and click "Forgot Password". A secure verification link valid for 15 minutes will be sent to your registered email address.
- Support representatives and automated agents cannot view, verbally dictate, or manually set customer passwords under any circumstances.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) & Lockouts
- For your protection, accounts are temporarily locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts for a duration of 30 minutes.
- If you have lost access to your 2FA authenticator device or registered phone number, account recovery requires identity verification with our Tier-2 security team.
